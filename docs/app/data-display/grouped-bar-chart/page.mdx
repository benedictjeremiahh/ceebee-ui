# GroupedBarChart <span className="docs__label">Composition</span>

Compare discrete bucket totals side by side on one zero-based scale. Unlike CashFlowChart,
this does not calculate a balance, net the series, or imply a continuous trend between buckets.

## Usage

<GroupedBarChartDemo />

## Interaction and accessibility

Hover or focus a group to read every series at full precision. Upstream Tooltip owns focus,
positioning and dismissal. Groups are read-only, not buttons. A hidden table carries all values;
forced colors displays that table instead of the plot. There is no entry animation.

Missing, negative and non-finite values are unknown, not zero. Supply `formatExact` whenever
the axis formatter abbreviates. The component does not assume a currency or bucket dates.

<Guidance do="Compare nonnegative totals across a few categories or periods."
  dont="Treat due or accrued amounts as dated cash movements or a future balance." />

spacing and focus use CeeBee Tokens; the bar geometry comes only from the data.
