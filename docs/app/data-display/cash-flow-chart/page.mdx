# CashFlowChart <span className="docs__label">Composition</span>

Money in and out per period, with the running balance over them. `BalanceCurve` shows *that* cash dips;
this shows *why* — which week's bills pull it under and which receipt brings it back. It is the shape
cash-forecast dashboards converge on: inflow bars up, outflow bars down, the balance as a line across both.

## Start from the real balance

`opening` is the balance before the first period — today's cash. The line starts there and runs through
each period's close. Starting from zero draws a projection of *change*, which reads like a projection of
cash and is not one.

## What it states

Above the plot, one sentence gives the two figures a decision needs: the first period that closes below the
line and the lowest balance. A period that closes below the line is shaded, and the lowest point carries a
small dot with its figure, so the trouble reads by position and fill as well as by colour. The zero line is
drawn stronger than the grid, and axis ticks fall on round numbers.

## Periods are the consumer's

The chart does not bucket. Pass days, weeks or months, each with its `start` day, `inflow` and `outflow`
(positive; a negative outflow is read as the same amount). A period bucketed from finer figures can dip and recover
inside itself — a week that is overdrawn on Wednesday and paid on Friday closes above zero. Pass its `low`
(the lowest balance within it) and the chart reads that against the line, shades the period and marks the
dip, so a mid-period shortfall is never hidden by the close. `formatPeriod` names a period; by default it is
its start day in the document's language. With many periods, every *n*-th label is shown so none overlap.

## Interaction contract

Read-only unless `onSelectPeriod` is given. Then each period is a button named with its date and all three
figures, `aria-pressed` for `selectedPeriod`, so the consumer can list what is behind it beside the chart.

## Accessibility

A visually hidden table gives every period's in, out and balance; the drawing is `aria-hidden`. Under
`forced-colors: active` the bars and line keep system colours and the table comes into view; under
`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the bars do not grow into place.

## Tokens

`--cb-tone-success` fills inflows, `--cb-tone-danger` mixed with `--cb-fg-subtle` fills outflows,
`--cb-tone-brand` draws the balance and the low point, `--cb-tone-danger` shades a period below the line and
colours the breach sentence, `--cb-border` the grid, `--cb-fg-muted` the zero line and the axis text,
`--cb-focus-width` the focus ring, `--cb-radius-sm`, `--cb-space-*`, `--cb-text-xs`, `--cb-text-sm`, and
`--cb-duration-deliberate` with `--cb-ease-entrance` for the bars growing in.
