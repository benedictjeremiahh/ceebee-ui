# Leaderboard <span className="docs__label">Composition</span>

A ranked list. Server-safe, and an `<ol>` — the ranking is a fact a screen reader reads out, not a
column of numbers it has to infer from the layout.

## Usage

<LeaderboardDemo />

<Guidance
  do="Mark the viewer's own row with you, wherever it sits — finding yourself is the point of a leaderboard."
  dont="Use medal tints on a plain ranked table; gold on row one implies a competition that may not exist."
/>
