`CashFlowChart`: money in and out per period with the running balance over them. Inflow bars up, outflow bars down, the balance as a line from an opening balance; a period that closes below the line is shaded and the lowest point marked, and one sentence states when it goes under and how far. Periods become buttons when `onSelectPeriod` is given; a hidden table carries every figure.
