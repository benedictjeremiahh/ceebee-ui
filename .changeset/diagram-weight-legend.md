Diagram edges take a `weight` (0–1): the line thickens with the traffic it carries and its arrowhead stays the same size; a weight of 0 draws a path nothing has taken, dashed and quiet. `legend` lists explicit entries (shape, tone, meaning) so two pills can mean different ends, and legend swatches now render at their size. `Diagram` takes `fitMinZoom` for an overview that must show the whole diagram at once.
