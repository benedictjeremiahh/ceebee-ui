`Select` sizes itself to its options: the field is as wide as its longest option (12rem–28rem, never wider than its container), the dropdown may be wider than the field up to 32rem, and a value or option that still does not fit ellipsises with its whole text in a tooltip. A consumer width, `popupMatchSelectWidth`, `labelRender` or `optionRender` still wins.
